How I Work

A disciplined path from uncertainty to decision.

The process is designed to surface the issues that matter most before they become expensive, reputational or transaction-critical.

1. Define the decision Clarify what must be decided, by whom, and against which standard of confidence.

3. Translate the riskConvert technical and commercial information into investor, lender and insurer language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before we begin

What stage should a project or company be at?

Engagements can support early investor readiness, pre-diligence preparation, active project development or transaction-specific review. The scope depends on the decision that needs to be made.

Do you provide technical engineering certification?

No. I translate technical evidence into bankability and risk questions. Where specialist engineering, legal, insurance or market validation is required, that need is identified clearly.

Do you introduce investors?

Investor introductions are not the core service. The priority is to make the opportunity more credible, structured and ready for the right capital conversations.

Do you work on unpaid exploratory projects?

No. My work is focused on paid advisory engagements where an independent assessment can materially improve investment decisions, project bankability or technology positioning.

Is the work limited to VRFB?

No. VRFB and long-duration energy storage are central areas of focus, but the bankability framework also applies to other emerging energy and infrastructure technologies.
