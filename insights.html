Research Philosophy

Independent thinking. Evidence before advocacy.

My work begins with a simple observation: technology alone does not create financeable infrastructure. Capital requires confidence, and confidence is built through evidence, risk allocation, contractual clarity and commercial alignment.

Rather than asking whether a technology works, I ask whether investors, lenders and insurers have enough evidence to commit capital. That difference changes every conversation.
